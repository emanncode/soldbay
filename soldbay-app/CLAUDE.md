## Core Design Rules
- **Typography**: 
  - **All Headers / Titles**: `Bricolage Grotesque` (`font-display`, `BricolageGrotesque-SemiBold` / `-Bold`)
  - **All Paragraphs / Body / Non-Headers**: `Inter` (`font-sans`, `Inter-Regular` / `-Medium` / `-SemiBold`)
- **Global Color Code**: **Deep Forest Green** (`#3b7e68`) as global primary brand, Warm Off-White (`#fcfbf9`) background, Coral Red (`#df4a32`) accents.
- **Spacing & Layout**: Follow the 8-point grid, NativeWind tokens, and glassmorphic atmosphere.
