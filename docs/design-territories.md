## Comparison setup

The three proposals use the same representative discovery task and synthetic content: a buyer browsing the required AAUA · Akungba-Akoko campus scope, searching listings, and viewing Recently Added. The listing is “Calculus & Analytic Geometry,” ₦8,500, condition “Very good,” marked “Negotiable.” The second listing is “Graphing calculator,” ₦12,000, condition “Good.” Names, prices, campus, and sample listing photography are illustrative only; they are not real listings or user evidence.

The proposals deliberately vary information composition and browsing hierarchy as well as type and color. No new product behavior is introduced. They are concept screens, not final device-faithful screens, platform foundations, wireframes, or completed UI.

## Review and limitations

- The three screens share campus, search, categories, Recently Added/listing context, synthetic item details, and the same logical 390 × 844 viewport.
- They change information composition and image prominence, not product rules or workflows.
- The same stock sample image is used in all three concepts so image treatment can be compared consistently; it and all listing data are illustrative and must not be mistaken for Soldbay inventory.
- Pencil screenshots show the comparison and anchored bottom navigation without visible clipping. This canvas review does not establish accessibility conformance, device behavior, responsive adaptation, usability, or suitability of real listing photography.
- Status bar, network/battery indicators, home indicator, safe areas, and platform-specific system behavior belong to later device-foundation work. Current concepts are not final screen specifications.
