## 🛠️ 2. Key Features & Changes Implemented

### A. Role-Based Onboarding & University Selection Flow
- **Unified Signup Stage**: Both Buyer and Seller complete registration on `/signup` and proceed to the "Choose your University" stage (`/select-university`).
- **Buyer System (Skippable / Non-compulsory)**:
  - University selection is optional for buyers.
  - Added a top-bar **Skip** action with chevron icon and a prominent bottom **"Skip for now (Go to Buyer Home)"** button.
  - Tapping Skip immediately routes buyers to `/buyer/home` without requiring a campus selection.
  - Selecting a campus updates the profile and smoothly routes to `/buyer/home`.
- **Seller System (100% Compulsory / No Skip)**:
  - All skip buttons and bypass affordances are disabled and hidden for sellers.
  - Subtitle explicitly guides sellers that campus selection is mandatory prior to verification.
  - Selecting a campus saves the university association and forwards directly to `/seller/verify`.

### B. Scraping Physical ID Cards $\rightarrow$ Student Portal Screenshot Verification
- **Problem**: In Nigerian universities, physical student ID cards are rarely issued or renewed promptly at the start of new academic levels, causing sellers to hold outdated cards.
- **Solution**: Completely scrapped physical ID card verification in favor of **Student Portal Home Page Screenshot verification**.
- **Implementation**:
  - Requires a clear screenshot of the student's personal **Student Portal home page** (explicitly distinguishing from exam portals) showing full student name, matriculation/ID number, and university details.
  - Updated `/seller/verify` UI across upload zone, file picker, photo preview, submitting, and pending review states.
  - Updated backend verification route (`POST /api/sellers/verify`) with tailored validation, 5 MB file size limit, image format checks (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC), and storage under `seller-portals/`.

- **`POST /api/auth/signup`**: Gracefully accepts registration without requiring `universityId` upfront.
- **`PATCH /api/users/me`**: Updates `universityId` when selected, or leaves it null when skipped by buyers.

## 🔍 3. What We Figured Out & Key Learnings
1. **Domain Reality in Campus Tech**: Authentication workflows in African universities cannot rely solely on physical cards. Live student portal home pages provide verifiable, tamper-resistant, real-time proof of matriculation and level status.
2. **Friction vs. Discovery Balance**: Forcing buyers to choose a school before seeing products increases drop-off. Presenting the school picker with a clear 1-tap "Skip" preserves campus network effects for committed users while keeping discovery instantaneous for casual shoppers.
3. **Immersive Dark Glass Design Language**: Native OS alert dialogs break the visual immersion of dark glass UI. Standardizing on custom `ErrorBanner`, `ConfirmDialog`, and `ToastBanner` primitives ensures aesthetic cohesion across iOS, Android, and Web.
4. **Optimistic Route Parameters**: Passing role via route params (`useLocalSearchParams`) alongside backend `getMe()` verification eliminates flickering or delay during role-dependent screen rendering.
